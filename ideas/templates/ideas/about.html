A fair chance for a fresh thought

Good ideas don’t
need a famous name.

AnoRank is a space to share possibilities and consider them thoughtfully, regardless of who suggests them.

01 / SHARE

Put a possibility
into words.

Give your idea a title, choose a category, and explain what it could change. No account is needed and we don’t collect a name.

02 / CONSIDER

Look at it from
three angles.

Think about feasibility, impact, and originality. Rate each from 1 to 5. Different perspectives are welcome.

03 / DISCOVER

See what
resonates.

Explore new ideas or visit the leaderboard. Save what interests you and return to it with a fresh perspective.

A transparent approach

Three criteria.
Equal weight.

Every submitted rating counts equally. The community score is the average across all ratings and all three criteria, on a scale of 1 to 5.

( Feasibility + Impact + Originality ) ÷ 3

Example: 4 + 5 + 3 gives an overall score of 4.0.

Feasibility

Can it work with realistic resources, time, and effort?

Impact

Could it meaningfully improve things for people or the world?

Originality

Does it introduce a fresh approach or rethink something familiar?

A little thoughtfulness goes a long way

Our shared ground.

Give ideas a fair chance

Evaluate the idea itself. A low score isn’t a dismissal, and a high score isn’t a guarantee. Early ideas often need time to develop.

Keep personal information private

Submissions are public. Avoid names, contact details, or confidential information in titles and descriptions. AnoRank’s submission form does not associate an idea with a user account.

Understand your browser session

Saved ideas and a random rating identifier are linked to this browser session using a cookie. Clearing the cookie or switching browsers resets that connection. Ratings do not prove unique people and are not intended for consequential decisions.

Be constructive and respectful

Share ideas in good faith. Avoid harmful content, personal attacks, and copying someone else’s work. Site administrators can remove inappropriate submissions.
